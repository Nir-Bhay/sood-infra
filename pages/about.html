Our Journey: From Vision to Reality

Sood Infra Private Limited began its journey with a vision to deliver high-quality, affordable real estate solutions tailored to diverse needs. Established by Mr. Nitesh Sood and Mrs. Anuradha Sood in 2022, the company has quickly emerged as a trusted name in residential, commercial, and industrial real estate projects.

Inception

Company incorporated on November 10, 2022

First Project

Shri Naveen Chambers project in 2023

Building Trust

Achieved significant customer trust by 2024

Today, Sood Infra stands for excellence, innovation, and commitment in delivering projects that exceed expectations. From state-of-the-art residential properties to cutting-edge commercial spaces, we are shaping the future of real estate in Madhya Pradesh and beyond.
