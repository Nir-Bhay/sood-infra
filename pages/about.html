Our Journey: From Vision to Reality

Sood Infra Private Limited embarked on its mission with a clear vision: to provide high-quality, affordable real estate solutions that cater to a wide range of needs. Founded in 2022 by the dynamic trio, Mr. Nitesh Sood, Mrs. Sonia Verma, and Mrs. Anuradha Sood, the company has rapidly grown into a trusted name in the real estate industry. With a keen focus on residential, commercial, and industrial developments, Sood Infra strives to blend innovation with practicality, ensuring that each project not only meets but exceeds expectations. In just a short span, we’ve built a reputation for excellence, driven by our unwavering commitment to quality, customer satisfaction, and transformative spaces.

Our vision extends beyond the present, as we strive to redefine the real estate landscape in Madhya Pradesh and beyond, ensuring each project we undertake reflects our dedication to quality, functionality, and long-term value.
