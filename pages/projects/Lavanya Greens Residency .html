Welcome to Lavanya Greens Residency

नभ स्पर्श दीप्तम्

Proposed Residential Multi-storeyed Project (G+12 with G+2 Parking)

Learn More Contact Us

Project Overview

Lavanya Greens Residency is an upcoming G+12 multi-storeyed residential project featuring 160 spacious flats. This project is designed to cater to modern living needs, with a mix of 2BHK and 3BHK apartments, built with thoughtful planning and precision. The project is designed according to Vastu principles, ensuring positive energy flows through every unit.

  • Total Plot Area: 53,620 sqft.
  • Ground Coverage: 16,000 sqft
  • Open Space: 37,620 sqft – providing ample space for recreational activities, gardens, and more.

Lavanya Greens Residency is designed with the lifestyle of its residents in mind, offering a living experience that blends luxury, comfort, and community spirit.

Location & Accessibility

Strategically located, Lavanya Greens Residency is easily approachable and visible from the road. The complex is just a walking distance from Shri Naveen Chambers (a premium commercial space) and Anuradha Walk Street (a vibrant retail concept designed for residents), ensuring that everything you need is just a short walk away.

Building Specifications

The project will feature a G+12 structure with 2 levels of parking, designed to accommodate 160 cars and 160 two-wheelers. The building will house a total of 720 occupants, providing a balanced living environment for families, professionals, and individuals alike.

  • 160 Flats: A mix of 2BHK (80 units) and 3BHK (40 units)
  • 4 Lifts: Two for passengers, two for luggage
  • Emergency Escape Stairs
  • Towers Connected at 10th Floor: The two towers will be connected at the 10th floor, creating a passing channel with a glass tube walkway, offering breathtaking views and a unique architectural experience.
  • Parking Space: Ample parking for 160 cars and 160 two-wheelers

Amenities & Features

Lavanya Greens Residency offers a range of world-class amenities designed to enhance your lifestyle:

  • Clubhouse: For relaxation, socializing, and recreational activities
  • Landscaped Gardens: Ample open space for walking and outdoor activities
  • 24/7 Security & CCTV Surveillance: Ensuring peace of mind
  • Power Backup & Water Supply: For uninterrupted living
  • Sports & Recreational Areas: For residents of all ages
  • Community Spaces: Designed to foster a connected, active lifestyle

Price Range & Booking

Lavanya Greens Residency offers competitive pricing for its high-end apartments, starting at:

  • 2BHK Flats: Starting at ₹35 Lacs
  • 3BHK Flats: Starting at ₹45 Lacs

With the perfect balance of modern design, premium amenities, and strategic location, these homes are an ideal choice for anyone looking for a quality living experience.
