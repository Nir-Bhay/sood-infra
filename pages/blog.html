Local Media Coverage and Sources

Sood Infra's activities are frequently covered by several regional media outlets, which highlight the company's involvement in infrastructure and development projects in Chhindwara and Madhya Pradesh. Here are some key media sources to follow for updates:

  • Nayi Duniya: A prominent regional Hindi daily in Madhya Pradesh that often covers local news and regional developments. Look for press releases or feature articles related to Sood Infra’s projects and business activities.
    How to Use: Visit their website or search archives for terms like "Sood Infra", "Chhindwara Infrastructure", or "Madhya Pradesh construction" to find relevant coverage.
  • Madhya Pradesh Bhaskar: A leading regional news outlet that covers politics, economy, and regional development. You can find updates on state-funded infrastructure projects and the involvement of local companies like Sood Infra.
    How to Use: Search for features, op-eds, or news articles that discuss infrastructure development in Chhindwara, especially those mentioning Sood Infra. Editorials or reports on the impact of infrastructure on local communities are also valuable.
  • Chhindwara News (Local News Portals): Smaller, hyperlocal portals such as Chhindwara News and Chhindwara Samachar often provide updates on specific construction projects, government initiatives, and community events involving companies like Sood Infra.
    How to Use: Look for local updates about Sood Infra's work in Chhindwara. These could include announcements of new projects, government tenders, or CSR activities.
  • Dainik Jagran: Another major Hindi newspaper that serves the region, covering developments related to local businesses, infrastructure, and public-private partnerships.
    How to Use: Check for news about Sood Infra’s involvement in government initiatives or public-private partnerships in Chhindwara, which may be covered in Dainik Jagran.
