Our Journey: From Vision to Reality

Sood Infra Private Limited began its journey with a vision to deliver high-quality, affordable real estate solutions tailored to diverse needs. Established by Mr. Nitesh Sood and Mrs. Anuradha Sood in 2022, the company has quickly emerged as a trusted name in residential, commercial, and industrial real estate projects.

Inception

Company incorporated on November 10, 2022

First Project

Shri Naveen Chambers project in 2023

Building Trust

Achieved significant customer trust by 2024


Today, Sood Infra stands for excellence, innovation, and commitment in delivering projects that exceed expectations. From state-of-the-art residential properties to cutting-edge commercial spaces, we are shaping the future of real estate in Madhya Pradesh and beyond.

Our Mission, Vision, and Values

Mission

To deliver exceptional and sustainable real estate solutions by focusing on affordability, quality, and unmatched customer satisfaction. We aim to set new benchmarks in the real estate industry through ethical practices and continuous improvement.

Vision

To emerge as a leading real estate developer known for crafting cost-effective, aesthetically pleasing homes and modern commercial spaces. We envision fostering community growth and sustainability while inspiring trust and innovation in all our endeavors.

Values

  • Integrity: Upholding honesty and transparency in all our actions.
  • Customer-Centricity: Prioritizing customer needs to create meaningful experiences.
  • Innovation: Embracing creativity and technology to deliver unique solutions.
  • Timely Delivery: Ensuring projects are completed within promised timelines.
  • Sustainability: Promoting eco-friendly practices for a greener future.

At Sood Infra, our mission and vision guide every step of our journey as we build spaces that inspire and create value for communities and stakeholders alike.
