SLB (Sale Lease Back) Model Explained: How Buying a Shop Guarantees You Lifetime Rent in Chhindwara!

What is the SLB (Sale Lease Back) Model?

The SLB Model is a smart, hassle-free investment plan where you buy a property (like a shop), and the same company leases it back from you to manage and rent it out. In return, you earn guaranteed monthly rent for life without lifting a finger. It’s like owning a gold mine that never stops giving!

How Does the SLB Model Work? (Step-by-Step for Clarity):

Step 1: You Buy the Shop

  • Purchase a commercial shop in Shri Naveen Chambers Commercial Complex.
  • The shop is legally registered in your name – you’re the full owner.

Step 2: Company Leases It Back

  • After registration, Sood Infra Private Limited (the builder) signs a Lease Agreement with you.
  • We lease the shop back from you for lifetime rental management.

Step 3: Rent Starts Immediately

  • From the next month of registration, you receive rent directly in your bank account.
  • No waiting for tenants or paperwork – we handle everything.

Step 4: Lifetime Benefits

  • Rent continues as long as you live and can even be passed to your heirs.
  • The shop remains your asset – you can sell it later or leave it as a legacy.
