Welcome to Anuradha Walk Street

सहिता कार्यसाधिका

A High Street Retail Project serving Lavanya Greens Residency and the National Highway.

Learn More Contact Us

Project Overview

Anuradha Walk Street is the epitome of convenience and lifestyle, strategically designed to serve the residents of Lavanya Greens Residency and the bustling traffic from the National Highway. This G+4 retail complex features 96 versatile shops, providing everything from everyday essentials to unique lifestyle offerings. It is a vibrant space where business meets community, creating an ecosystem of interconnected growth and convenience.

Designed with the needs of the local community in mind, Anuradha Walk Street offers a wide array of retail shops catering to daily needs, lifestyle, and entertainment. With 96 shops of 12x12 (144 SQ.FT.) and multiples, this retail complex ensures that everything is within easy reach. Whether you're looking for groceries, medical supplies, or simply want to enjoy a meal or movie, Anuradha Walk Street has you covered.

  • Plot Area: 11,500 SQ.FT.
  • Ground Coverage: 5,000 SQ.FT.
  • Parking Area: 6,500 SQ.FT.

Anuradha Walk Street is not just a retail space; it’s a dynamic hub that connects the commercial, residential, and recreational aspects of this ecosystem.

Shops Available

The complex is designed to house shops for a variety of services, from grocery stores to food outlets. The following businesses will be part of the development:

  • Grocery Stores & Milk Shops
  • ATM & Mobile Recharge Services
  • Haircutting Salons
  • Medical Stores & Doctor Chambers
  • A Little Cinema Theatre for entertainment
  • Restaurants & Ice Cream Parlours
  • Small Cafes & Fast Food (Burgers)
  • Clothing & Footwear Shops
  • Optical Stores & Mobile Shops
  • ...and much more!

Strategic Location & Accessibility

Anuradha Walk Street enjoys a prime location, positioned just 80 feet away from both Shri Naveen Chambers and Lavanya Greens Residency. This perfect location connects the National Highway directly to the heart of Lavanya Greens, making it easily accessible to both the residents and the passing traffic.

The Ecosystem of Growth: All Phases Connected

At Sood Infra Private Limited, we believe in building spaces that foster connectivity and community. The ecosystem of Phase 1: Shri Naveen Chambers, Phase 2: Lavanya Greens Residency, and Phase 3: Anuradha Walk Street forms a cohesive, interconnected environment where each phase complements the other:

  • Phase 1: Shri Naveen Chambers – A pure commercial project that anchors the area with high-quality business spaces.
  • Phase 2: Lavanya Greens Residency – A pure residential development, offering modern homes and a lifestyle centred around comfort, luxury, and convenience.
  • Phase 3: Anuradha Walk Street – A vibrant retail complex that serves as the heartbeat of the community, providing essential services and entertainment, ensuring that every resident and visitor’s needs are met.

Together, these projects create a holistic ecosystem where every phase connects seamlessly, allowing people to work, live, and thrive in harmony. The concept of " जीवो जीवस्य जीवनम् " (Life is interconnected through life) resonates through this design – where the commercial, residential, and retail spaces come together to create a balanced and thriving environment for all.

Book Your Shop at Anuradha Walk Street

With strategic location, modern infrastructure, and a diverse range of businesses, Anuradha Walk Street offers the perfect opportunity for retailers and entrepreneurs to set up shop in a thriving community. Whether you’re looking to start a new venture or expand an existing one, this retail complex offers a dynamic and vibrant environment.
