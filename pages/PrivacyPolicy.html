Privacy Policy

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information when you interact with our website or services:

  • Personal Information: Name, email address, phone number, postal address when you fill out contact forms or request information about our projects.
  • Project Information: Details about properties you're interested in or inquiries about our construction services.
  • Technical Data: IP address, browser type, device information, and website usage data through cookies and analytics tools.
  • Communication Data: Records of your correspondence with us via email, phone, or contact forms.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your inquiries about our real estate projects and construction services
  • Provide personalized information about properties that match your interests
  • Improve our website and services based on user interactions
  • Send project updates and marketing communications (with your consent)
  • Process bookings or reservations for property viewings
  • Comply with legal obligations related to real estate transactions

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We may share your information with:

  • Our authorized real estate agents and construction teams to serve your needs
  • Legal authorities when required by law or to protect our rights
  • Third-party service providers (e.g., CRM systems, email platforms) who assist in our operations
  • Financial institutions for mortgage or payment processing when applicable

4. Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your personal information, including:

  • Secure servers and encryption for sensitive data
  • Limited access to personal information within our organization
  • Regular security audits of our systems

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access, correct, or delete your personal information
  • Opt-out of marketing communications
  • Request information about how your data is being used
  • Withdraw consent for data processing (where applicable)
