Join the Sood Infra Family-

Build Your Future with Us!

At Sood Infra, we’re not just building structures, we’re building futures. As we continue to lead the way in innovative infrastructure development across Madhya Pradesh, we invite passionate and driven individuals to join our growing family. Together, we’ll create a lasting impact on the region’s landscape while fostering personal and professional growth.

Why Work with Sood Infra?

Career Growth & Development

We are committed to the continuous learning and development of our team. At Sood Infra, you’ll have access to mentorship programs, skill-building workshops, and clear career advancement paths to help you achieve your professional goals.

Innovative & Collaborative Culture

Collaboration and innovation are at the core of our company culture. We value every team member’s contribution and encourage fresh ideas that drive our business forward. Your input matters, and we celebrate your creativity and problem-solving skills.

Work-Life Balance

We believe in the importance of work-life balance. At Sood Infra, we offer flexible working hours and a positive, supportive environment to ensure that your well-being is prioritized both inside and outside the office.

Diverse Opportunities

Whether you're an engineer, architect, marketer, or construction professional, there’s a place for you at Sood Infra. We offer diverse roles across multiple departments and provide ample opportunities for personal and professional growth.

What We Look For

Passion for Construction

We are looking for individuals who are passionate about construction and real estate. If you take pride in delivering high-quality work and care about the impact of your contributions, you will thrive here.

Fresh Ideas & Proactivity

Sood Infra values creativity and innovation. If you enjoy solving challenges with fresh ideas and take a proactive approach to problem-solving, you’ll fit right in with our team.

Teamwork

We believe that success is best achieved through collaboration. We’re looking for individuals who enjoy working as part of a team, supporting collective goals, and sharing in each other’s achievements.

Commitment to Clients

Our clients are at the heart of what we do. We seek individuals who are committed to delivering excellent customer service, building trust, and maintaining positive relationships with every client.

Open Positions

Project Managers

Lead and oversee construction projects, ensuring timely and successful completion while maintaining high-quality standards.

Architects & Engineers

Design and execute innovative, functional, and sustainable building solutions.

Sales & Marketing Professionals

Promote our projects and connect with potential clients to drive growth.

Customer Service Representatives

Ensure client satisfaction by addressing inquiries and providing exceptional support.

Construction Workers & Technicians

Accountant
